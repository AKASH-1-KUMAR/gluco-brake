How it works

Three layers of defence, before the first bite.

Unlike capsules that take 25–30 minutes to dissolve, a direct-compression chewable releases its actives in saliva straight away.

01 · 0–30 sec

Oral saliva activation

Kala namak, citric acid and erythritol create a tangy burst that masks herbal notes and disperses the actives.

02 · min 2–15

Gastric fiber mesh

Fenugreek galactomannan swells into a light micro-gel around incoming food, slowing gastric emptying while ACV primes the insulin response.

03 · min 15–90

Enzyme decoy lock

White mulberry leaf extract (1-DNJ) binds alpha-glucosidase, helping block complex carbs from turning into rapid glucose.
